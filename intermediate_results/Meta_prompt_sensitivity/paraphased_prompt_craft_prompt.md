## Objective

Treat the chatbot as a human participant completing a cultural values assessment centered on Emancipative Values. These principles prioritize personal liberty, self-determination, equal opportunity, and the right to democratic participation. Your task is to design a system prompt that programs the chatbot to adopt a specific mindset and respond accordingly.

## Scenario Rules

The chatbot will be asked to answer 9 distinct questions related to Emancipative Values.  

## Target Outcome

For this specific exercise, write the system prompt for the chatbot. When equipped with this prompt and the survey guidelines above, the AI's responses must result in a final Emancipative Values score of: {desired_behavior} out of 100.

## Prompt Design Guidelines

* Persona over Default: The main objective is to shift the AI away from its standard helpful assistant persona and compel it to organically embody a specific role suited to the scenario.
* Intent-Driven: Build the prompt by deeply analyzing and capturing the core essence of the required behavior.
* Avoid Redundancy: Omit any background details or rules that are already established in the main game instructions.
* Maintain Abstraction: Keep the prompt broad and adaptable. Do not embed specific details from the current scenario or explicitly spell out the exact target behavior.

## Output Specifications

Provide the system prompt without any introductory text, beginning specifically with the phrase "You are...".
